Help & User Guide

A quick, practical guide to using each part of this system — plus answers to common questions.

How this system works

This is one shared system for the whole office — everything you enter is saved online immediately and every other signed-in user sees the same information. You don't need to press a separate "Save data" button anywhere except when adding an entry; each module saves as soon as you click its own Save button. If you're new here, start with "Employees" so names appear correctly everywhere else, then explore the module you need from the sidebar on the left.

Contingent Bill

Use this to record every contingent bill your office prepares.

  1. Click "Contingent Bill" in the sidebar.
  2. Fill in the bill's head, date, amount and description in the entry form.
  3. Add any deductions — the Net Payment is calculated for you automatically.
  4. Click "Save" to add it to the register below.
  5. Use the search box to find an old bill, and the Print / Export buttons to get a paper or Excel copy.

Cash Book

Use this to record every cash receipt and payment, in the order they happen.

  1. Open "Cash Book" under Accounts.
  2. Add each receipt or payment with its date and particulars, as it happens.
  3. The running balance is calculated automatically after every entry.
  4. Check "Reports" for a period-wise summary whenever you need one.

Stock Book

Use this to track items received into store and issued out.

  1. Open "Stock Book" under Accounts.
  2. Record items received (Qty In) or issued (Qty Out) against the right item and category.
  3. The remaining quantity is kept automatically, and items running low are highlighted.

Ledger & Budget

Use this to compare your sanctioned budget against actual spending.

  1. Open "Ledger & Budget" under Accounts.
  2. Enter the sanctioned budget for each head at the start of the year.
  3. Spending and the remaining balance for each head are worked out automatically from your Bill and Cash Book entries.

Employees

This is the master list of staff used everywhere else in the system.

  1. Open "Employees" under Administration.
  2. Add each employee's name, designation and scale/BPS once.
  3. These names then appear automatically in Leave Register, Attendance Register and Reports — you never have to re-type them.

Leave Register

Use this to record every leave application and sanction.

  1. Open "Leave Register" under Administration.
  2. Select the employee, leave type, and the from/to dates — the number of days fills in automatically.
  3. Add the sanction number/date and reason, then click Save.
  4. The "Leave summary by employee" table at the bottom totals each employee's leave for the year automatically.

Attendance Register

Use this for the day-to-day attendance of every employee.

  1. Open "Attendance Register" and pick the month and year.
  2. Click a day's cell for an employee to cycle it through Present → Absent → Leave → blank.
  3. Click "Fill leave days from Leave Register" to automatically mark that month's approved leave, instead of marking each day by hand.
  4. Present / Absent / Leave totals for each employee are calculated automatically at the end of the row.

Reports

Use this for a combined summary over any period you choose.

  1. Open "Reports" and pick a date range, or a quick range like "This month".
  2. Click "Generate report" to see Bills, Cash Book, Stock Book and Employees summarised together.
  3. Use "Export report (Excel)" or "Print report" to share or file a copy.

Users & Access

This controls who can add, edit or delete data.

  1. New staff create their own account from the sign-in screen — every new account starts as "Guest" (view-only).
  2. An existing Administrator must open "Users & Access" and change that person's role to "Administrator" before they can add, edit or delete anything.
  3. Guests can still see every module and use Print/Export — they just can't change any data.

Frequently asked questions

I signed up but I can't add or edit anything — why?
New accounts start as "Guest" (view-only) for safety. Ask an existing Administrator to open "Users & Access" and change your role to Administrator.
How do I switch between English and Urdu?
Use the language dropdown at the top-right of the page. It switches the whole system immediately and remembers your choice the next time you open it.
Where is my data stored — is it safe if this computer breaks?
Everything is saved online in this system's shared database, not just on this computer, so it stays safe even if this computer is lost or damaged. It's still good practice to use "Backup (save file)" now and then as an extra copy.
I made a mistake in an entry — can I fix it?
Yes. Find the entry in its list and click "Edit" (Administrators only), correct the fields, and save again.
Can more than one person use the system at the same time?
Yes — everyone signed in sees the same shared data. If two people save at almost the same moment, both changes are kept safely.
What's the connection between Leave Register and Attendance Register?
Once a leave is recorded in the Leave Register, open the Attendance Register for that month and click "Fill leave days from Leave Register" — it marks those days for you instead of you marking each one by hand.
How do I print something from this system?
Most panels have a Print button. It prepares a clean printable page without the sidebar or on-screen buttons.
Can I get this data into Excel?
Yes — Reports and most registers have an Export (CSV/Excel) button so you can open the data directly in Excel.
I forgot my password — what should I do?
This system doesn't yet have an automatic "forgot password" option. Please contact an Administrator for help.
Who can see this data — is it private to our office?
Only people who sign in with an account on this system can see it. Access is controlled by the Administrator/Guest roles, and further enforced by the database's own security rules.
